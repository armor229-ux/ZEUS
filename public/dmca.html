DMCA Notice & Takedown Policy

How to report copyright concerns to ZEUS. We respond to all valid takedown requests in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

Last updated: January 2026

ZEUS Does Not Host Any Content

ZEUS is an informational discovery platform. We do not host, upload, store, stream, or make available any video files on our servers. All movie and TV show metadata (titles, posters, descriptions, ratings) is provided by The Movie Database (TMDB) API.

Video playback on ZEUS is delivered through third-party embed players hosted by independent providers. When you press play, your browser connects directly to those external services — for example vidsrc.to, vidsrc.cc, vidsrc.su, multiembed.mov (SuperEmbed) and player.autoembed.cc (AutoEmbed). We do not control these providers, their libraries, or their content, and we are not affiliated with them in any way. If content appears on one of those third-party players, it exists on their infrastructure, not ours.

How to Submit a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright holder (or an authorized agent acting on behalf of one) and you believe content accessible through ZEUS infringes your copyright, you may submit a takedown notice. Follow the steps below so we can process your request quickly and completely. Incomplete notices may delay or prevent action.

  1. Identify the copyrighted work Describe the original work you believe has been infringed — for example the movie or TV show title, and where the authorized version is officially distributed (official site, streaming service, or distributor).
  2. Identify the infringing material Provide the exact ZEUS URL(s) where the material can be accessed (e.g. https://zeus.example.com/watch.html?id=...). Include enough detail for us to locate the specific page and the third-party server involved.
  3. Include your contact information Full legal name, company (if applicable), mailing address, telephone number, and the email address at which you can be reached.
  4. State your good-faith belief Include a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Declare accuracy and authority Include a statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Sign and send the notice Send the completed notice, including a physical or electronic signature, to our DMCA contact address below. Email is the fastest channel.

DMCA Contact

Submit all takedown notices and copyright inquiries to:

zeus.dmca@gmail.com

Please include “DMCA” in the subject line.

Please allow up to 3–5 business days for a response. Valid notices result in the removal of the referenced link from ZEUS.

What We Can and Cannot Remove

Because ZEUS only links to third-party players, we can remove the link or embed from our pages, but we cannot delete the underlying video file — that content lives on the third-party provider's servers, outside of our control. For complete removal, we recommend that copyright holders also send takedown requests directly to the hosting providers listed above, as well as to their ISPs and search engines.

Repeat infringement policy: links associated with repeated valid takedown notices are permanently removed from the site, and we cooperate fully with rights holders who provide bulk notice lists.

Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to the same email address with the same contact details, the URL(s) removed, a statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was a mistake, and your consent to the jurisdiction of your local courts. We will review it and, where appropriate, restore the referenced link.
