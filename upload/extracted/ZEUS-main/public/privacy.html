Privacy Policy

How ZEUS handles (and does not collect) your data. The short version: no accounts, no sign-ups, no personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

1. No Accounts, No Personal Data

ZEUS has no user accounts, no registration and no sign-up forms. At no point do we ask for, collect, store or process personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, payment details or date of birth. You can browse, search and play content on ZEUS entirely anonymously.

We also do not run our own analytics, advertising networks, tracking pixels or fingerprinting scripts on this site. The site is a static front-end: your browser fetches pages and public movie data (from the TMDB API), and that is the whole story.

2. Local Storage (Watchlist & Preferences)

The only data ZEUS saves lives inside your own browser, in a feature called localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. We use it for:

  • Your Watchlist — the movies and TV shows you save with the heart button are kept in your browser under the key streamverse_watchlist, so your list is still there the next time you visit.
  • Disclaimer dismissal — once you close the disclaimer banner at the top of the page, we remember that choice (key streamverse_disclaimer_dismissed) so it does not appear again.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings ("Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data"), or by using the Clear All button on the Watchlist page.

3. Third-Party Embed Players

Video playback is provided by independent third-party embed services (for example vidsrc.to, vidsrc.cc, vidsrc.su, multiembed.mov and player.autoembed.cc). When you open the watch page, an iframe connects your browser directly to those providers.

These providers are separate companies and may collect their own data — such as your IP address, device information, or cookies — in accordance with their own privacy policies. ZEUS does not control that collection and is not responsible for it. If you prefer to avoid third-party data collection entirely, do not open the video player. Also note that Google Fonts (used for typography) receives your IP address to serve font files.

4. Cookies Notice

ZEUS itself sets no tracking cookies. We only use localStorage as described above, which is technically similar to a cookie in that it is stored on your device, but it is never sent to our servers with requests. Third-party embed players may set their own cookies when you press play, as noted in the previous section.

5. TMDB API

Movie and TV metadata (titles, posters, overviews, ratings, cast lists) is fetched live from The Movie Database (TMDB) API. Your browser sends requests to TMDB's servers to retrieve this public data. TMDB may log those requests like any web server does. ZEUS is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

6. Children's Privacy

ZEUS is a general-audience discovery site and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no personal data is collected at all, no such data is stored or shared. Parents concerned about third-party players should supervise playback, as those services operate under their own policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Since the site collects no personal data, significant privacy changes are not expected.
